### 💡 Why Pennywise?
Legacy financial software traps families inside proprietary vendor silos and black-box calculators. We are architecting a new paradigm:

* **Household Data Sovereignty:** Families own their canonical financial record, keeping sensitive data portable, private, and decoupled from closed SaaS vendors.
* **Portable Lifetime Context:** Clients seamlessly grant scoped access to accountants, estate lawyers, or wealth advisors while retaining their complete historical record—eliminating the friction of starting from scratch with every new professional.
* **Empowered Scenario Validation:** Individuals can model complex outcomes, test proposed strategies from financial providers against their own baseline, and validate decisions with unshakeable mathematical rigor.
* **Conflict-Free Clarity:** Uniting exact tax and cash-flow logic with natural AI scenario synthesis so households can act with total conviction.
